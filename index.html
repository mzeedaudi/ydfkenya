Who we are

A youth-led force for change

Youth Development Forum (YDF) is a youth-led nonprofit based in Kibera, Nairobi. We give children and young people in Kenya’s informal settlements the education, skills and opportunities they need to build brighter futures.

A future where every child and youth in Kenya’s informal settlements rises above poverty through equal access to quality education, opportunity, and dignity. Our vision
