The role of sport in education

Sport builds discipline, teamwork and good health. Here’s why it belongs at the heart of every school.

Sport plays a vital part in a well-rounded education. Beyond keeping children fit, it teaches discipline, teamwork, resilience and leadership: qualities that help young people grow, in school and in life.

Better learners

In many schools, making sport part of the timetable has improved students’ concentration, self-esteem and social skills.

Healthier, and more likely to stay in school

Sport supports mental well-being, encourages healthy habits and can help reduce school dropout. For talented young athletes, it can also open doors to scholarships, careers and community leadership.

Educating the whole child

At YDF, we believe education isn’t only about books. It’s about developing the body, mind and character. When sport is a key part of school life, young people thrive both in and beyond the classroom.
