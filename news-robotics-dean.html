Ushirika School students explore robotics with DEAN

Pupils at Ushirika School got their first hands-on lesson in robotics, thanks to the Digital Education Africa Network (DEAN).

Students at Ushirika School have taken an exciting step towards digital inclusion. Through a partnership with the Digital Education Africa Network (DEAN), they were introduced to the world of robotics.

Learning by doing

In an interactive session, learners worked with real robots. The experience sparked their curiosity and built early skills in STEM: science, technology, engineering and maths.

It also showed why it matters to prepare young minds for a future shaped by technology.

Closing the digital divide

YDF is proud to support learning like this, which helps bridge the digital divide and gives young people the confidence to innovate.
