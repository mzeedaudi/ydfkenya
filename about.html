About us

Empowering youth. Inspiring change. Building the future.

Youth Development Forum (YDF) is a dynamic, youth-led initiative that unlocks the potential of young people in vulnerable communities.

We believe young people aren’t just the leaders of tomorrow. They’re the changemakers of today.

YDF gives young minds a place to engage, grow and lead. With the right support, every young person can become a force for positive change in society.

What drives us

Our mission and vision

Our mission

To empower underprivileged children and youth in Kenya’s slums by providing transformative education, life skills, and economic opportunities that unlock their full potential and create lasting change in their communities.

Our vision

A future where every child and youth in Kenya’s informal settlements rises above poverty through equal access to quality education, opportunity, and dignity.

Our principles

What we stand for

YDF was founded on three principles that guide everything we do.

01

Empowerment

We give young people the tools, skills and confidence to shape their own futures.

02

Inclusion

Every young person deserves a place to engage, grow and lead, whatever their background.

03

Sustainable development

We work for lasting change in communities, not quick fixes.

What we do

Six ways we open doors

  1. Quality education

    Schooling in Kibera from early childhood (ECDE) to senior secondary.

  2. Scholarships

    For bright young students, with 120 awarded every year.

  3. Skills training

    Practical training that prepares young people for work.

  4. Economic empowerment

    Support for small and medium-sized businesses in the slums.

  5. Career guidance

    Helping young people choose a path and plan their next steps.

  6. Job placements

    Connecting young people with real job opportunities.
